CRITICAL QUALITY ATTRIBUTE EXCURSION:
Physical characterization and QC testing of finished crystalline API batch AMX-2026-1102 failed Dissolution Testing at the 15-minute timepoint (71.4% dissolved vs release specification limit: NLT 85.0%). 
Particle size distribution (PSD) by laser diffraction revealed coarse particle agglomerates with D90 = 142µm (Spec: D90 < 110µm).

INVESTIGATION DETAILS:
Review of automated SCADA crystallization profiles for Crystallizer CR-402 indicated that during the controlled pH precipitation phase, the variable-frequency drive (VFD) agitator slowed from 180 RPM down to 95 RPM for 42 minutes due to an inverter thermal warning.
This drop in agitation energy caused localized high super-saturation near the reagent inlet nozzle, inducing excessive secondary nucleation and agglomeration.
Maintenance logs show the VFD motor cooling fan filter was clogged with dust and had not been cleaned during the last quarterly PM.
